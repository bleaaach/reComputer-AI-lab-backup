# Hardware Connection
This page describes the required cables and basic connection order.
Before starting with your reComputer RK3576, please follow the steps below to connect your peripherals:

## Connect Input Devices
Plug your keyboard and mouse into the USB 2.0 or USB 3.0 ports on the device.
<img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reComputer_RK/wiki_image/connect_5.jpg" width="600" />

## Connect a Display
Connect your monitor to the HDMI port using an HDMI cable.
<img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reComputer_RK/wiki_image/connect_4.jpg" width="600" />

## Connect to the Network
Insert a network cable into the Gigabit Ethernet port for a stable internet connection.
<img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reComputer_RK/wiki_image/connect_7.jpg" width="600" />

## Connect Power
Finally, plug the power adapter into the DC Jack. The device will power on automatically.
<img src="https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/reComputer_RK/wiki_image/connect_3.jpg" width="600" />
